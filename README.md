# **Global Mobile Prices 2025 – Power BI Dashboard **

## **1. Introduction**

This report summarizes the insights extracted from the Global Mobile Prices 2025 Extended dataset using an interactive Power BI dashboard. The goal was to understand brand pricing strategies, feature-driven price patterns, and overall market positioning. The analysis supports product planning, competitive benchmarking, and customer segmentation for companies operating in the mobile phone market.


## **2. Dataset Overview**

The dataset includes these key fields:

* **brand, model**
* **price_usd**
* **ram_gb, storage_gb, camera_mp, battery_mah**
* **display_size_inch, charging_watt**
* **5g_support**
* **os (Android/iOS)**
* **processor**
* **rating**
* **release_month, year**

These attributes allow complete trend analysis, correlation studies, and market comparison across brands and features.


## **3. Dashboard Structure**

The dashboard was designed for clarity and quick decision making. It includes the following major sections:

### **3.1 KPI Summary**

Four high-level indicators appear at the top:

* **Average Price (USD)**
* **Average RAM (GB)**
* **Average Storage (GB)**
* **Average Rating**

These KPIs give an immediate snapshot of the market and help track performance.



### **3.2 Slicer Panel (Filters)**

A clean slicer panel was added to improve interactivity and allow targeted analysis:

* Brand

This filter help different teams focus on the segments relevant to them, such as premium brands, new launches, or 5G-supported devices.


### **3.3 Trend and Brand-Level Insights**

The central visuals include:

#### **A. Price Trend by Brand (Line Chart)**

Shows how average prices evolve over time for each brand.
This helps identify:

* premium vs budget brand strategies
* price stability or volatility
* launch cycles and innovation timing

#### **B. Average Price by Brand (Bar Chart)**

Compares brands based on their average pricing.
Useful for market positioning and competitive comparison.

#### **C. Average Rating by Brand (Column Chart)**

Shows customer satisfaction patterns across brands.
Reveals if premium pricing is justified by higher ratings or if budget brands are outperforming expectations.


## **4. Correlation Analysis**

Four scatter plots were created to evaluate how specifications influence price:

### **4.1 Price vs RAM**

Higher RAM generally pushes the price upward, showing RAM is a strong value indicator.

### **4.2 Price vs Storage**

A clear positive trend, confirming storage capacity impacts pricing decisions.

### **4.3 Price vs Camera MP**

Shows whether brands charge more for higher megapixel cameras and how much customers value camera performance.

### **4.4 Price vs Battery mAh**

Battery capacity is typically less correlated with price, which helps companies avoid over-investing in a low-impact feature.

These charts help product teams understand which features justify higher costs and which do not.



## **5. Feature Pricing Summary**

A matrix visual compares brands and feature levels using **Average Price**.

This matrix answers questions like:

* Which brand charges the most for high-end RAM or storage?
* Where do brands compete or differentiate?
* Which features contribute the most to premium pricing?

This supports feature prioritization, cost management, and competitive pricing decisions.



## **6. Key Insights**

From the dashboard, companies can draw several actionable insights:

* **Brand Positioning:**
  Premium brands maintain higher average prices and often score better in ratings.

* **Feature-Driven Pricing:**
  RAM, storage, and camera quality show strong correlation with price.
  Battery capacity shows weaker influence.

* **Market Segmentation:**
  5G models and larger storage variants contribute to higher price brackets.

* **Launch Timing Patterns:**
  Certain months show higher model releases, helping plan marketing and production schedules.

* **Customer Satisfaction:**
  Some mid-range brands achieve strong ratings, revealing opportunities in the value segment.



## **7. Conclusion**

The Power BI dashboard provides a complete view of the mobile market in 2025, combining pricing trends, feature correlations, and brand comparisons. This analysis can help companies refine product strategies, optimize pricing, and understand customer preferences. It also supports better planning, marketing, and competitive positioning in a rapidly evolving mobile market.
